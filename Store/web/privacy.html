Privacy policy

This HTML page is an unhosted draft. The published, current policy is SubGen's privacy policy on GitHub, updated October 10, 2026. Use that policy for the App Store listing.

Local transcription and files

After setup, whisper.cpp transcribes your audio locally. FFmpeg and FFprobe prepare media and process subtitle tracks locally. The app writes generated subtitles and, when requested, a verified video with embedded subtitles to your Mac.

Translation and ChatGPT sign-in

The Mac App Store version lets you authorize an eligible ChatGPT account in your browser. It receives identity and authorization information from OpenAI and sends subtitle text, the target language and translation instructions directly to OpenAI for translation. Audio and video are not uploaded. Requests use store:false; this setting does not replace OpenAI's processing, retention or account policies. Translation uses your ChatGPT plan or credits and is subject to availability and limits. The app does not operate a translation proxy or analytics backend.

OAuth credentials are saved in this app's macOS Keychain item. Your selected account registration and a stable, random installation UUID are saved locally. Credentials and authorization URLs are excluded from app diagnostics. Logout clears local credentials and attempts to revoke the connection with OpenAI; the app explains if server revocation cannot be confirmed. Local identity and registration information remains for reconnecting the same account.

The direct-distribution version instead invokes your separately installed Claude Code or Codex CLI, which communicates with its configured provider. Its authentication, telemetry, retention and service terms depend on your CLI configuration.

Downloads

The Mac App Store version includes its media and speech executables and downloads speech model data from Hugging Face. The direct version also downloads tool archives from GitHub and OSXExperts. Download services and their delivery networks can receive IP addresses and ordinary request metadata.

Local preferences and diagnostics

The app stores preferences locally; the store version saves file access bookmarks for its queue and authorized output folders. It also writes local diagnostic logs that may contain file paths or process error messages. Logs are rotated after approximately 5 MB. Review and remove private information before sharing any logs for support.

Your choices

You choose the files to process and whether to translate or embed subtitles. You may cancel a task. Local models and installed tools remain on your Mac until you remove them. Removing the app alone may leave those separately downloaded files and local preferences.
